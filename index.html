The Dihedral Angle of a Trihedral Angle

Find the angle θ between two faces using only the three face angles α, β, γ

cos θ = cos γ − cos α · cos βsin α · sin β

🔺 Setting

Three rays OA, OB, OC leave a vertex O and form three faces. θ is the dihedral angle between the red and blue faces, measured along their common edge OA.

Red face AOB: ∠AOB = α Blue face AOC: ∠AOC = β Green face BOC: ∠BOC = γ (the "opposite" face)
